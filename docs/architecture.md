## Beach & environment (`scenes/beach.tscn`, `scripts/beach.gd`, `assets/`)
Everything visible is a node or resource in `scenes/beach.tscn` (editable in the editor): `WorldEnvironment` (`assets/env/beach_env.tres`: custom sky material `assets/materials/sky.tres`, ACES, glow, SSAO, fog + volumetric fog), `Sun` DirectionalLight3D (shadow settings), `Terrain` (MeshInstance3D, `scripts/terrain.gd`), `Water` (PlaneMesh + `assets/materials/water.tres`), `DuneGrass` (Node3D, `scripts/dune_grass.gd`, 6 MultiMeshInstance3D children), `Boardwalk` (`scenes/props/boardwalk.tscn`), `PlayerStart` (Marker3D on the boardwalk; the player spawns there facing the sea), `Props` (10 `scenes/props/palm.tscn` + 10 `rock_cluster_a/b/c.tscn` instances, positions set by hand/generator, y from height_at), `Lighthouse` (`scenes/props/lighthouse.tscn`: Poly Pizza glb scaled x2, ~17 m, + warm OmniLight3D lamp; at x=-62, z=2 on a headland with 3 `LighthouseRocks` clusters; base collider), and `Player` (instance of `scenes/player.tscn`). Meshes are baked `.res` in `assets/meshes/` (terrain, palm trunk/frond, rocks, `grass_clump_a..d` / `grass_tuft_e,f`, `boardwalk`; scattered grass instances in `assets/meshes/grass_mm/`); materials in `assets/materials/` (sand, water, grass, sky = ShaderMaterial on `assets/shaders/`; leaf, trunk, rock = StandardMaterial3D with vertex colour).
- `Beach` (`scripts/beach.gd`, `@tool`, group `beach`): `_ready` applies the look, connects `GameState.health_changed` and calls `_spawn_gameplay()` (DebrisManager, Shack, HUD, Menu, Cheats, Wildlife; still script-built). Editor: select Beach and move the `health` slider (or edit a look `.tres`) to preview the look live; the runtime-only stuff is skipped in the editor.
- Look = `BeachLook` resource (`scripts/beach_look.gd`, `@tool`) = one time-of-day keyframe with a `health` position: sun arc (`sun_arc_deg`: 0 = horizon behind the player at -Z, 90 = overhead, 180 = sea horizon at +Z; `sun_tilt_deg` rotates the arc off the dune-sea axis), sun colour/energy, sky colours + `sky_glow` + clouds, fog, ambient, saturation/contrast/glow, water (+ `water_glitter`), sand, grass, palm tints. Beach exports `looks: Array[BeachLook]` = `assets/env/look_morning.tres` (0), `look_midday.tres` (0.5), `look_afternoon.tres` (0.8), `look_sunset.tres` (1.0). `set_health(t)`: `look_at_health(t)` blends the two neighbouring keyframes linearly with `BeachLook.mix()` (lerps every exported float/Color, so the sun angle moves along the arc), then `_apply_look()` writes sun direction, sky material (the sky shader reads `LIGHT0_DIRECTION`, so the disc always matches the DirectionalLight; it is clipped at the horizon so the sunset sun is half-sunk), environment, water (`sun_dir` + `glitter` drive the sun glitter path) and the sand/grass/leaf materials.
- `Terrain` (`scripts/terrain.gd`, `@tool`) owns `shore_z(x)` and `height_at(x, z)` (Beach delegates; safe to call before any `_ready`) and the noise (`get_noise()`). Mesh is baked to `assets/meshes/terrain.res` (1 m grid, x -70..70, z -80..70; sea +Z, dunes -Z, shoreline near z~0, headlands at x ends); inspector button "Bake mesh" regenerates it after editing `height_at`. Trimesh collision (`TerrainBody`) is created at run time. `DuneGrass` has "Bake clumps" (6 meshes of 14-90 thin curved blades, vertex colour = dryness/phase/stiffness, UV.y = root..tip) and "Scatter" (6000 clumps + 1300 tufts; dense where zs < -21..-29 below the shoreline, bare patches by noise, sparse short tufts on the upper sand, clear of the shack rectangle and the Boardwalk corridor; per-instance custom data = dryness, phase) buttons; `tools/bake_dunes` runs both plus the boardwalk bake offscreen (needs a real renderer: MultiMesh buffers are empty under --headless). Re-run after changing terrain or the path. `Boardwalk` (`scripts/boardwalk.gd`, @tool): `path_points` (x,z) polyline (default = `Terrain.PATH`, beach end first, x~-10, z -18..-34.5), "Bake mesh" builds terrain-following planks, posts and rope into `assets/meshes/boardwalk.res` (planks have no collider: the player walks on the terrain under it; the fence does, see Collisions). `Player` takes its start x/z/yaw from the `PlayerStart` marker (`--pos` overrides). Palms/rocks are not re-placed automatically.
- Shaders: `sand` (colour patches + warm/cool drift, near-camera blotch/mottle, flat-beach wind ripples vs contour-following ripples on dune slopes (slope from the geometry normal), grain normals that fade with distance, darker smoother wet band with wavy edge following `swell` + a dried salt line, dune/grass-line tint; uniforms `sand_dry/sand_light/sand_wet/grass_tint/swell` unchanged), `water` (vertical depth colour from the depth texture; swell+chop vertex waves; foam bands; 4 scrolling ripple-normal layers (2 broad + 2 fine, fine ones fade out 25-110 m); shallows refract the screen texture (`refract_strength`) with a depth tint and fade to nothing at the waterline (no hard sand edge); Fresnel + a built-in screen-space reflection (18-step view-space march on the depth texture + 3 refine steps, `reflect_strength`, 0 = off) so palms/lighthouse/rocks reflect, and where the ray misses the engine's sky reflection (SPECULAR ~0.16) is the fallback; sun glitter path = tight lobe x sparkle noise + a broad sheen; the transparent pass reads `hint_screen_texture`, so ALPHA is 1 and the shader blends refracted scene/body colour itself). **Vertex wave formula (copied by floating debris, unchanged)**: `swell_at(w) = sin(T*0.55 + w.x*0.05)*0.6 + sin(T*0.31 + w.x*0.11 + 1.7)*0.4`; `chop = sin(w.x*0.3 + T*0.8)*cos(w.z*0.37 - T*1.1) + 0.5*sin(w.x*0.71 - w.z*0.53 + T*1.7)`; world water height = `Water.y + swell_amp*swell_at(w) + chop_amp*chop` with `swell_amp` 0.12, `chop_amp` 0.06, T = TIME, w = world position. `sky` (gradient, sun glow/disc, cartoon clouds), `grass` (travelling world-space gust waves bending blades more towards the tips plus small fast flutter, per-blade/clump green-to-straw dryness, darker root / lighter tip, backlight translucency; uniforms `root_color`/`tip_color` set by BeachLook, others have shader defaults).
- API: `set_health(t: float)` (0 = fresh morning, sun low behind the dunes; 0.5 = midday; 0.8 = golden afternoon; 1 = sunset, sun half-sunk on the sea horizon; never night). Starts at health 0 (or the saved health). `height_at(x, z) -> float` gives ground height (use for spawning debris/props). `shore_z(x) -> float` gives the shoreline z at x. Dev: user arg `--health=0.7` overrides the start value.
- Player (`scenes/player.tscn`, `scripts/player.gd`): CharacterBody3D with CollisionShape3D (capsule), `Head` (y 1.6) and `Camera3D` (fov 75) nodes; `beach` = its parent. Instanced in beach.tscn, so it exists before `_spawn_gameplay` runs.
- Perf (tools/fps, vsync off, offscreen, RTX 3070 Mobile 1600x900; machine shared with other agents so +-15%): sea views ~350-450 fps, dunes/start ~230 fps. Environment SSR was tried (ssr_enabled, 24 steps): -35% fps on the sea view and no visible gain (only wet sand is glossy) so it stays off; the water does its own reflection. `tools/fps [scene] [user args]` prints the average fps (dev arg `--fps=SECONDS`).

## Dunes, boardwalk end, collisions, truck (dunes/truck task)
- `Terrain.height_at`: dune ridges start ~15 m behind the shore (`ramp`), crests meander along the shore (sin of zs + x with noise warp, `ridge` pow 1.5) and reach ~5-7 m with flanks up to ~35-40 deg (p90 ~29 deg in the playable area; check with a script before pushing amplitudes). Kept low: the path channel (`PATH` polyline + `CHANNEL_TAIL`, flat within `CHANNEL_IN` 3.5 m, full dunes beyond `CHANNEL_OUT` 13 m, closes behind the truck at z -49..-60), a pad around the shack (`SHACK_PAD`) and the truck parking spot (`PARK`, flat at `PARK_Y`). `Terrain.path_dist(x, z)` is public. After editing height_at: `tools/bake_dunes` (bakes terrain, boardwalk, grass), then re-snap props (palms sit at height_at - 0.1; rocks/lighthouse keep their old offset to the ground) and re-place the truck (`Props/PickupTruck`, y from the ground at its axles).
- Boardwalk end: the beach end (first `swallow_length` 4.5 m of the path, ~where the dense grass starts) is swallowed by sand: planks sink 2-12 cm, tilt, vanish at random, turn sandy, a `Drift` MeshInstance3D (`assets/meshes/boardwalk_drift.res`, shared sand material, bumps up to 10 cm fading out) covers them; fence posts/rope only start after it.
- **Collisions** (all in the prefabs, layer 1 so the player and the aim ray hit them; grass has none): boardwalk `Fence` StaticBody3D (`Shape` = trimesh `assets/meshes/boardwalk_fence_shape.res` baked with the mesh: 20 cm post boxes, 10 cm rope boxes), `palm.tscn` `Body/Trunk` capsule (r 0.32, 3.4 m, leaning), `rock_cluster_a/b/c.tscn` `Body` with one simplified convex hull per boulder (`tools/add_rock_colliders.gd`), `lighthouse.tscn` `Body/Base` cylinder (r 2.3, 7 m), shack `Hut/Body` box (existing), truck `Body` (two boxes). Test: `tools/tests/t12_test.tscn` prints `T12 PASS` (drives the Player with move_and_slide: fence/palm/rock/lighthouse/truck/shack stop it, grass and the walk do not; palms sit on the ground).
- **Pickup truck** (`scenes/props/pickup_truck.tscn`, Quaternius pickup via `tools/polypizza`, CC0): glb rotated 180 deg so the front points to -Z (inland); body mesh swapped for `assets/meshes/pickup_truck_open.res` (closed tailgate triangles removed by `tools/open_tailgate.gd`); `Cargo` holds the gear glbs (`assets/models/gear/`: cooler, bucket, rake, trash bags, 2 gloves, clipboard; credited in CREDITS.md). Parked at x -10, z -38.1 in `Props`, tailgate (+2.5 m from its origin) toward the player 3-4 m behind `PlayerStart`; the grass scatter keeps a rectangle around it clear (`DuneGrass._near_truck`). The Player clamp (z >= shore-33) and the dunes close the back; the truck is the visible edge.

## Gameplay (tasks 3-4)
- `GameState` autoload (`scripts/game_state.gd`): `cents` (money, integer cents), `pending` (cents of a bag still being sorted), `bag_capacity` (space units, float, 10), `bag` (Array of {id,value,space}), `cleared_count`, `initial_count`, `health()` = cleared/initial (0..1). Methods `bag_used()`, `bag_value()`, `can_add(space)`, `add_to_bag(id)` (emits `bag_full` and returns false if no room), `add_money(n)`, `take_bag()` (empties the bag, value to `pending`), `pay(n)` (adds money, emits `sold`), `sell_bag()` (take+pay, no animation: tests), `set_initial_count(n)`, `register_clear()` (tide pieces count too, capped at initial). Signals `bag_changed`, `money_changed`, `health_changed(v)`, `bag_full`. Task 5 below.
- `Player` (`scenes/player.tscn` + `scripts/player.gd`, CharacterBody3D, group `player`, instanced in beach.tscn): WASD (physical keys), mouse look, captured mouse (Esc releases, click recaptures), head bob. Stats vars `reach` (2.5), `move_speed` (5.0) for upgrades. Clamped to x +-50, z in [shore-33, shore+14]; the sea side is limited by the wading depth (see Wading & net). Every physics frame a ray (mask terrain+debris) sets `target` (debris, bottle or coin within reach, with the HUD label) and calls `set_highlight`; left click -> `_try_pickup()`. Dev args `--pos=x,z,yaw_deg[,pitch_deg]`, `--pitch=DEG`.
- `Catalog` (`scripts/catalog.gd`, preload, no class_name): THE data table. `ITEMS` (id -> name, space, value cents, bin, weight), `BIN_ORDER` (bin ids in emptying order; name/colour/icon are exports on the `Bin` instances in shack.tscn), `COINS` (loose-change values/weights), helpers `random_id`, `random_coin`, `money(c)` ("$12.34"), `price(c)` ("10¢" or "$1.50"), `num(x)` (6.5 / 10); scene lookups `item_scene(id)`, `coin_scene(cents)`, `BOTTLE_SCENE`.
- **Item prefabs** (`scenes/items/`): `<id>.tscn` for the 10 debris types, `coin_5/10/25/100.tscn`, `message_bottle.tscn`, `sand_puff.tscn`. Debris prefab = StaticBody3D (`debris.gd`, exported `id`, layer 2, group `debris`) > `Visual` (VIS_SCALE, sunk by `rest`) > `Model` (`scripts/item_model.gd`, scale = per-item real-size factor, exports `rest`, `can_stand`, colour `variants`/`accents` material lists + the parts they apply to) > `Pose` (lying pose) > MeshInstance3D parts (primitive meshes as sub_resources). Materials are shared `.tres` in `assets/materials/items/` (colour variants e.g. `cap_red.tres`, `outline.tres` highlight, `sparkle.tres`, `puff.tres`; shader `assets/shaders/item_outline.gdshader`). Prefabs were generated once from the old code; now edit them in the editor. `Catalog.VIS_SCALE` is re-applied at runtime to `Visual` (the baked 1.4 is only for editor display).
- `ItemMesh` (`scripts/item_mesh.gd`): `build(id, rng, upright=false) -> Node3D` plain model of one item at real size (meta `rest` = centre height): instances the prefab and keeps only its `Model` (random colour variant; `upright` stands bottles/cans/caps/flip-flops up). Used by the bin icons and the flying copies while sorting; API unchanged.
- `Debris` (`scripts/debris.gd`, StaticBody3D, layer 2, group `debris`): create with `Debris.spawn(id, rng) -> StaticBody3D` (instances `scenes/items/<id>.tscn`, random colour variant, yaw, tilt), then add_child and place. `id` (exported), `value`, `space` (set in `_ready` from `Catalog`); drawn `Catalog.VIS_SCALE` (1.4x, one uniform factor) life size, partly sunk; pick sphere baked in the prefab = max(`MIN_PICK_RADIUS`, size-based). `label_text()` ("Soda can · 10¢"), `set_highlight(on)` (`outline.tres` as `material_overlay`), `pick_up(cam)` (pop up, shrink toward camera, `sand_puff.tscn`, queue_free), `refuse()` (shake), `pop_in()`.
- `LooseChange` (`scripts/loose_change.gd`, StaticBody3D, layer 2, group `coin`): 5¢/10¢/25¢/$1 coin prefabs (`coin_<cents>.tscn`, exported `cents`), create with `LooseChange.spawn(cents, rng)`; twinkling sparkle quad; Player adds the money instantly (`add_money`), tick sound, never in the bag, no health effect. Not saved (10 respawn on load, tide sometimes brings one).
- `DebrisManager` (`scripts/debris_manager.gd`): instances the item prefabs with natural placement (no even spacing): `_wrack_line` (1250 pieces along a wavy high-tide mark ~3.4 m behind the shoreline, gappy, `WRACK_WEIGHTS` = small stuff + nets), `CLUSTER_COUNT` (260) gaussian clusters of 3-16 (mixed items drawn from the normal spawn weights), `DUNE_FOOT_CLUSTERS` piles where the sand meets the grass, `_rock_piles` around rocks standing on dry sand, `LONER_COUNT` single pieces, and `_floating_all` (FLOAT_NEAR 200 within waist-deep reach, FLOAT_FAR 320 out to 25 m, in drifting groups, `FLOAT_WEIGHTS` = light items only). A 1 m cell grid enforces `GAP` (0.3 m) spacing; exclusions: underwater, shack, boardwalk/dense grass (`_in_grass`), rocks and palm trunks (`_blockers`). Plus 10 loose coins; a 90 s Timer calls `tide()`: `TIDE_MAX` 36 -> `TIDE_MIN` 12 pieces as health rises, as 2-5 piece clusters on the wet sand, plus one 2-piece floating group in the shallows (40% chance of a coin); total capped at `MAX_ALIVE` 4600. `scatter(n)` (cheats) drops small clusters. A saved piece whose ground is under water is restored floating. Dev arg `--showcase` lays out one of everything in front of the default start.
- **Debris performance** (~4000 pieces): every debris mesh has `cast_shadow` off and `visibility_range_end` = `Debris.CULL_DIST` (45 m); floating pieces update their wave ride every frame only within 20 m of the camera, every 3rd frame to 50 m, every 8th beyond (staggered, `_skip`). fps (offscreen, tools/fps): default view 153 -> 110, midday view 229 -> 127 with 5x debris (unoptimised 42 / 73).
- **Plastic bag model**: `tools/models/bag.py` (`blender -b -P tools/models/bag.py`) writes `assets/models/items/bag.glb` (56 tris, flat shaded, one material, two handle loops); `scenes/items/bag.tscn` instances it under Pose; colour variants apply to its mesh node `Pose/Bag/Bag`.
- **Floating debris**: `Debris.set_floating(true)` (property `floating`): `_process` puts the piece on the wave surface (`scripts/water_wave.gd`, a CPU copy of the water shader's vertex wave: swell + chop with the amplitudes of `assets/materials/water.tres`; **keep it in sync with `assets/shaders/water.gdshader`**, TIME = engine ms / 1000), tilts it with the local slope, sits it partly submerged (centre about at water level), drifts it a few cm/s. Same aim interface as beach pieces (click within reach); counts for beach health like any other piece. No sand puff when picked from the water.
- **UI as scenes**: all UI lives in `scenes/ui/*.tscn` (Control nodes laid out in the editor, unique-name `%` nodes) with ONE shared `assets/ui/theme.tres` (StyleBoxFlat panels/bars/buttons + Label/Button/RichText type variations: HudPanel, ShopCard, MenuCard, PaperCard, RowPanel, BuyButton, PaperButton, ...). Scripts hold behaviour only. Add `load()` (not `preload`) of scenes whose scripts touch autoloads, or `tools/check` goes RED.
- `HUD` (`scripts/hud.gd` + `scenes/ui/hud.tscn`, CanvasLayer, group `hud`): rounded panel (`Bag 6.5 / 10` + fill bar that warms toward full, money `$0.00`, health % + bar), crosshair that grows a ring when `set_aiming(true, text)`, with a small label under it (name + value) from the target's `label_text()`, "Bag full!" flash + panel shake, "Click to play" when mouse released.
- `tools/check` now also boots the main scene headless for 30 frames (autoload-aware) and ignores `--check-only`'s false "Identifier not found: <Autoload>" errors.

Dev args `--open-panel`, `--sort-demo` (sell ~1.55 s after start; combine with `--fill`).
- **Sorting bins**: four box bins (Cans blue, Bottles green, Glass amber, Other orange) (`scenes/shack/bin.tscn`), each with a swinging lid, dark mouth and a plate with an item icon + name. `sell() -> cents`: `GameState.take_bag()`, group items by `Catalog` bin, plan a timeline of timers (`_at`): per bin in `BIN_ORDER` its lid opens (`_lid`), items fly out of the bag position as small `ItemMesh` copies (`_launch`/`_fly`: arc + shrink), land with `Sfx.bloob(step)` (pitch rises along a pentatonic scale, capped; at most ~14 sounds) and a bin squash, lid closes; finally `_finish_sort` calls `GameState.pay` (cash sound via `sold`, coin burst, money counts up). - HUD: money counts up (about 0.6 s) with a scale pop and floating "+$0.25"; `set_prompt(text, hold)` shows the bottom prompt.

## Wading & casting net
- `Player.depth` = sea depth at the feet (`water_depth(x, z)` = max(0 - terrain height, 0); the terrain trimesh also forms the seafloor, so there is collision under the water). Walk speed x (1 - 0.5 x smoothstep(0.05, 1.0, depth)), head bob x (1 - 0.6 x that), steps call `Sfx.splash` + `scenes/fx/wade_splash.tscn` (CPUParticles3D) instead of the sand crunch once depth > 0.05. Soft limit: from 0.8 m the speed component towards deeper water (terrain-height gradient) is removed progressively and a small push-back applies beyond `WADE_DEPTH` 1.0 m; z safety clamp is shore+14. First time at waist depth with >= 3 floaters further than reach and no net: toast "Buy a casting net at the shack to reach debris further out" (`GameState.net_hint_shown`, saved).
- Upgrade `net` (`GameState`): `NET_RANGE` [0, 12, 18, 22] m and `NET_RADIUS` [0, 2, 3, 4] m by level, `net_range()` / `net_radius()` (0 = not owned), base cost 1200.
- `NetCaster` (`scripts/net_caster.gd`, Node3D added to the Beach by Player; visuals are prefabs `scenes/net/net.tscn` (unit-size rope net + rim + buoys, shader `assets/shaders/net_mesh.gdshader`), `scenes/net/net_target.tscn` (flat landing ring on the water, `assets/shaders/net_ring.gdshader`, per-instance `tint`: white ok, red = not over water) and `scenes/fx/net_splash.tscn`). Right mouse held (mouse captured, net owned) = AIM (ring follows the crosshair on the sea, clamped to the range); release = FLY (~0.5-0.9 s arc, net spreads to its diameter) -> `_land_net` (splash + sound; floating debris within the radius goes to the bag nearest first via `can_add` / `add_to_bag` / `register_clear`; leftovers stay and the HUD flashes "Bag full!") -> REEL (1 s, net closes and drags the catch to the player) -> `_finish` (each piece flies into the bag with `Debris.pick_up`). Only floating pieces are caught. Dev args `--net-demo` (cast and freeze mid-air), `--net-aim` (show the ring).
- Tests: `tools/tests/t12_test.tscn` (counts, clustering, floaters on the surface, seafloor collision, wading depth limit, net catch/leftovers/health credit); t5 covers the net upgrade.

## Restoration (task 6)
- `GameState.MILESTONES` (id/at/msg: crabs 25%, gulls 50%, turtle 75%, hatchlings 100%), `reached` list, signal `milestone_reached(id)` emitted once per id from `register_clear()`.
- Beach: `GameState.health_changed` -> `_on_health_changed` tweens `set_health` over 2 s (sine). `set_health` moves the whole day (sun arc, sky, fog, water) between the keyframes. Dev: `tools/daycycle_shots 0 0.5 0.8 1` writes `.logs/day_<h>_sea.png` / `_dunes.png`.
- `Wildlife` (`scenes/wildlife/wildlife.tscn`, root script `scripts/wildlife.gd`, instanced by beach which sets `beach` var; group roots are scene nodes Crabs/Gulls/Turtle/Hatchlings). Each animal is a prefab in `scenes/wildlife/` (`crab`, `gull`, `turtle`, `hatchling`, `nest`.tscn; shared materials `assets/materials/wildlife/*.tres`); the script instances them and drives animation by finding named parts (Wing0-1/WingTip, Head, Flipper0-3; crab: `Body` node). Animals: crabs (5, ping-pong along the waterline; `crab.tscn` instances `assets/models/wildlife/crab.glb`, a single-mesh CC-BY model without animations, scaled 0.00064 and turned 180 deg; waddle bob/tilt on `Body` driven procedurally; credited in CREDITS.md), gulls (4, circling over the sea, glide/flap), turtle (1 at x=3.5, head bob + flippers), hatchlings (8 + sand nest at x=-3, walk to the sea, shrink into surf, loop). Everything animates in `_process` from `_t`; no collision (cannot block the pickup ray). Per-group `_ap` factor scales animals in (1.4 s, back-ease) on `milestone_reached`; on load groups at/below `max(GameState.health(), beach.health)` appear instantly (so `--health=1` shows all).
- HUD `toast(text)`: centre-top cream pill, shown on `milestone_reached`, fades after ~3 s.
